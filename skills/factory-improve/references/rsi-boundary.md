# RSI boundary (factory-improve)

## Fixed-rule improve (this skill) — **not** outer RSI

- Improves **tools / projects / skills** under **fixed** doctrine: dual-gate, no auto-promote, critic ownership, invent thresholds  
- Process skills (LFG, gauntlet, this triage) are **not** free to rewrite their own gates  
- Human / budget stop = **negative feedback** (slack on positive “improve more” loops)

## Outer RSI (deferred — doctrine D8)

- Self-directed rewrite of **how aims are chosen**, **proof meaning**, or **improve policy**  
- Always-on unattended compounding without human pattern + goal-proven evidence  
- Promoting sandbox self-improve policy into live factory without human  

## One-liner

> **RSI is when the improver may change the rules of improvement; factory-improve only changes tools under fixed rules.**
