# Creativity Engine

Everything starts with a conceptual metaphor.

Generate the core idea as a clean metaphorical combination first, in the style of:
- Starbucks is McDonalds for coffee.
- Netflix is UPS/FedEx for Blockbuster.
- Spotify is a gym membership for music.
- Costco is Netflix for groceries.

The metaphor should capture a key structural or experiential aspect so people grasp it instantly.

Then expand using the following frames and techniques.

## Core Creative Frames

Apply these systematically (or select the most relevant):

1. **Collapsed Advanced Civilization**
   Imagine a hypothetical past civilization more advanced than our own that completely collapsed and was erased from history. It is now rebuilding. Explore what caused their downfall and what the future looks like if we successfully avoid those same mistakes.

2. **Multiple Hypothetical Futures**
   Generate one or more distinct possible futures for the topic.

3. **Alternate History Futures**
   Imagine a hypothetical future in which key events turned out differently. Emphasize a positive end state even if negative or difficult events occur first.

4. **Future Predictions**
   Make concrete predictions about how this domain or idea evolves.

5. **Compare & Bridge**
   Compare and contrast the different futures or scenarios. Then ask: What would we need to create in order to bridge the gap between these futures?

6. **Alien Developmental Asymmetry**
   Imagine an alien world that advanced extremely far in some domains while remaining primitive in others. Some aspects far beyond us, others far behind. Explore the resulting civilization and technologies.

7. **Physics Plausibility Filter**
   After generating ideas from the above frames, consider only what is plausible under known physics. Modify or discard anything too impossible unless you can describe a mechanism or its precursors that could be developed starting today (even if very hard).

## Additional Brainstorming Techniques

When the topic is more free-form or pure brainstorming:

- List random things and free associations related to the core metaphor.
- Use graph-style thinking or workflow mapping to mind-map connections.
- Explicitly list worst possible outcomes, then invert them.
- Question core assumptions aggressively.
- Apply madlibs-style templates: take a solid sentence or concept and systematically swap key words/ideas with reasonable or interesting alternatives to generate variations.

## Process

1. Begin with the conceptual metaphor.
2. Run relevant frames from the Core Creative Frames list.
3. Apply additional techniques as needed.
4. Apply the Physics Plausibility Filter.
5. Synthesize the most interesting, coherent, and actionable directions.

Keep the thinking fluid, associative, and multi-threaded. Prefer surprising connections over safe linear progressions.
